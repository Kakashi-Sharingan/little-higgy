# Upstream attribution

Little Higgy customizes Open Higgsfield AI from https://github.com/Autom8AI/Open-Higgsfield-AI . The upstream README identifies its license as MIT and credits the original project and Muapi.ai. Original component sources are retained in packages/studio/src. The Little Higgy interface and local engine adapter replace the hosted-generation path; they do not include or grant access to proprietary AI models.

Dependency licenses remain with their respective authors. Locally installed AI models have their own licenses; consult each model's official source.
